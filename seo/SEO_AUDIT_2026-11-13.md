Live site, www.shopersite.in. All 95 sitemap pages were fetched. Checks and scoring follow the
claude-seo checklist (github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo); its scripts were not installed or run.
Scores are estimates from these checks, not a guarantee of rankings.

## Findings by priority

### High (fix this week)
1. **Themes page is empty for search engines.** Raw HTML has no text and no heading; the content only appears after scripts run. `src/app/themes/page.tsx`.
2. **Homepage heading doesn't say what Shopersite is.** "Your shop, online in 60 seconds" has none of the words people search for; the title does, the heading should too.
3. **Legal pages have no canonical** (`/legal` and all 13 documents). `src/app/legal/`.

### Medium (this month)
4. **Titles over 65 characters** on 16 pages (all 8 landing pages, About, Explore, 6 guides and help pages).
5. **Descriptions** over 165 characters on 8 pages, under 70 on 8 pages.
6. **27 help pages under 300 words.** Expand in batches, most-used first.
7. **Sign-up page in the sitemap** with no text or heading. Remove it from the sitemap.
8. **Pricing has no structured data of its own.** Add the same SoftwareApplication offers as the homepage.
9. **Homepage photos:** 181 without width and height, all from Unsplash. Serve the first screen from our own domain and add sizes.

### Low (backlog)
10. **HowTo structured data** on guides: Google stopped showing HowTo rich results in 2023. Harmless; leave or remove.
11. **llms.txt** missing. No major assistant has confirmed reading it; optional.

### Off-site (most of the remaining gain)
- Directory listings with the same description: AlternativeTo (in progress), Product Hunt, G2, Capterra, SaaSworthy, Startup India.
- Instagram, LinkedIn and X pages, then add them as `sameAs` in the Organization data.
- Real seller reviews and two or three seller stories.
- Bing Webmaster Tools (import from Search Console).

## Passed

HTTP to HTTPS and bare to www redirects (308, one hop); trailing slash removed; unknown pages 404; HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy present; no mixed content; no URL over 100 characters; homepage 203 KB with JSON-LD near the top; about 1,200 elements on the homepage; all search and AI crawlers allowed; no duplicate titles or descriptions; nothing in the sitemap is noindex; seller shops have their own sitemap and canonical.
